# What the Reddit and LinkedIn corpus is describing

This is a synthesis of 1,296 deduplicated public records (1,295 Reddit records and 260 LinkedIn records before deduplication). Theme counts overlap: one post can describe several problems. The corpus is query-selected, so the numbers indicate recurring signals in this collection rather than the share of all ADHD or neurodivergent people.

## The lived problems

**Starting and restarting work is a breakdown in activation, not a lack of knowledge.** Posts describe knowing what needs to happen but being unable to cross the first step, especially after an interruption, context switch, missed detail, or an unfinished task has become emotionally heavy. Time blindness, working-memory loss, open loops, procrastination, and “stuck” states make ordinary planners feel like another obligation. The requested help is usually concrete: remember where to resume, reduce a task to one action, provide a prompt, or make the first step visible.

**Work creates a second problem: translating an internal need into a safe request.** People describe meetings that erase details, ambiguous expectations, deadline friction, follow-up they cannot initiate, and the cost of masking. Accommodation can mean written instructions, quiet or flexible focus time, clearer priorities, or a way to ask without disclosing a diagnosis. The fear is not only embarrassment; it is being judged as unreliable, difficult, or less capable, with possible career consequences.

**Capacity fluctuates and the environment can consume it before the task starts.** Burnout and masking posts describe cumulative depletion rather than ordinary tiredness. Sensory load, bright lights, noise, social performance, poor sleep, and repeated switching can leave someone unable to do a task they could complete on another day. The useful response is pacing: a smaller version of the task, permission to pause, lower-demand options, and recovery without a failure score.

**Rejection and criticism turn ordinary friction into a threat response.** RSD and stigma posts describe anticipating criticism, over-explaining, avoiding feedback, and spending energy preventing rejection. The product implication is a neutral interface that does not grade effort, shame an abandoned task, or frame support as correcting a defective person.

**Access and treatment logistics are real pain, but they are a different product problem.** Posts mention diagnosis waitlists, cost, uneven access, medication shortages, and uncertainty about where to start. These signals support better access information and referral research; they do not justify making a diagnostic or treatment product from this corpus.

**Existing systems fail when their maintenance cost exceeds the user’s available capacity.** Tool and routine posts are full of resets: a system works briefly, then collapses when energy, context, or schedule changes. The repeated design requirement is low setup, flexible granularity, and a graceful exit. A full planner, streak system, or always-on assistant would reproduce the burden people are describing.

## What each platform contributed

LinkedIn contributed 260 text-bearing records. Its material is more often polished workplace, advocacy, accommodation, clinical-access, and neuroinclusion content. It is useful for vocabulary and organizational constraints, but many records are educational or promotional rather than first-person accounts.

Reddit contributed 1,295 text-bearing records across ADHD, ADHD programmers, ADHDers, ADHD women, autistic adults, autism, executive dysfunction, and neurodiversity communities. It contains more first-person accounts of burnout, shame, diagnosis/access frustration, relationship strain, and day-to-day work failure. Because Reddit is much larger in this collection, raw totals are not a fair platform prevalence comparison.

The platform split supports a product that works for both contexts: private enough for workplace use and concrete enough for a personal “I am stuck right now” moment.

## Why the first product wedge is resume support

The broadest themes are work/accommodation (455), stigma/identity (370), burnout/masking/energy (296), and executive function/task initiation (274). The narrower concept scan found 271 resume-friction records (124 LinkedIn, 147 Reddit), 87 containing explicit support/request language, and 60 containing privacy-risk language. Energy/sensory pacing is adjacent and nearly as large (266 records); it should shape the first version’s skip, snooze, and low-energy behavior rather than be ignored.

Resume support is the smallest useful product boundary that connects these problems without claiming to solve them all: it addresses activation and interruption, can be private by default, and can accommodate variable energy by letting the user choose a smaller step or stop. The recommendation is a proxy build hypothesis. It still needs scripted engineering checks now and consented participant evidence before high-confidence claims about usefulness.

## Data quality cautions

The collection includes cross-query duplicates and some reposted or promotional LinkedIn content. Theme labels are heuristic and overlapping. Representative excerpts in the raw analysis are evidence links, not quotes from interviewed participants. Do not report any count as “X% of neurodivergent people.”
