## Explicit limits

Phone dimensions and touch input are emulated. Reduced viewport height represents keyboard space, not a real phone keyboard. Full standalone installation is tested in desktop Chromium, not on a physical Android or iOS home screen. No screen-reader session, participant comparison, payment test, public deployment, or clinical benefit is claimed. The colour check covers primary body text, not every possible UI state. Backups are unencrypted, imports have a 10 MB limit, and browser clearing can remove saved data.

Installation references: [MDN installability guide](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Progressive_web_apps/Guides/Making_PWAs_installable) and the [Chrome DevTools Protocol source](https://github.com/ChromeDevTools/devtools-protocol/blob/master/json/browser_protocol.json), inspected on 15 September 2026. These describe platform behaviour and test commands, not evidence of user benefit.
