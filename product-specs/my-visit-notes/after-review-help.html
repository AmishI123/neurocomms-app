Record what happened

Open Review after the visit. Choose Answered, Partly answered, Still unclear or Not discussed. These are your notes, not a medical conclusion. Opening the review does not mark anything done.

Your answer note is personal and is not confirmed by a clinician. It stays private unless you choose to include it in the review. It never appears in conversation view automatically.

Make a next step

Choose Turn this into an editable next step. The app starts with the question's words; it does not interpret your answer or create a medical instruction. Edit the words, add an optional date and contact, then save the visit. The question and its original visit stay separate.

Review and continue

Review what happened and what comes next includes only the questions, personal notes, communication requests and next steps you choose. It does not include private preparation, source context or history. Copying, downloading and printing do not save, send or mark anything complete. If copying fails, download the private review.

Save successfully before closing. Saved visits can return offline after setup and can move to another browser through the normal private transfer. Unclear items can be selected deliberately for the next appointment. There is no automatic sending, scheduling, cloud recovery or syncing.
