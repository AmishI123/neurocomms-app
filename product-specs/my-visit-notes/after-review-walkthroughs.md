# Twelve fictional after-appointment journeys

These are software examples, not participant evidence or real appointments.

1. **Answered clearly.** Alex marks one question Answered, writes a personal note and saves. The original question is not shared unless selected.
2. **Partly answered.** Mina chooses Partly answered and leaves a note for later. The item remains unfinished and can be carried forward.
3. **Still unclear.** Sam chooses Still unclear. The saved internal state remains Ask later for older follow-up tools, while the review shows the plain label.
4. **Not discussed.** Lee leaves a question Not discussed. Opening review does not mark it answered.
5. **Make a next step.** Rae turns a question into a next step, edits the starting words, adds a date and contact, then saves it separately.
6. **Private review.** Jo selects a personal note in the private review. It appears only in that review and never in conversation view automatically.
7. **Short export.** Chris downloads the exact review. The file does not contain private preparation, source context or history, and does not change state.
8. **Clipboard failure.** Nico cannot copy the review. The app offers a download and keeps the editor unchanged.
9. **Interrupted save.** Pat loses storage access while editing. The review stays in the page and says it is not saved; retry works later.
10. **Offline return.** Dee saves halfway, closes the browser after offline setup and reopens the same outcomes, notes and next steps offline.
11. **Another device.** Fran transfers the saved visit, reviews it on another browser and carries the unclear question to a future appointment. The sender remains unchanged.
12. **Next visit.** Ash answers one copied question in the next appointment. The earlier question and its answer remain separate; only deliberate follow-up work moves forward.

Private data stays in the browser or chosen private file. No automatic sending, medical interpretation, scheduling, account or native-app claim is made.
