# After-appointment review

Status: active, not released. A1–A7 define the full goal.

## A1 — Say what happened

For every question, offer Answered, Partly answered, Still unclear and Not discussed. These are the person's notes, not a medical conclusion. Existing saved states map to the plain labels without changing old visit meaning. Nothing becomes answered because the person opened the review, viewed a question, made a summary or downloaded a file.

## A2 — Keep personal answers private

The answer field says it is personal and not confirmed by a clinician. It stays out of conversation view and shared summaries unless explicitly selected. Private preparation, answer notes and follow-up history never appear in a short review by accident. Literal text and blank lines remain exact and safe as text.

## A3 — Make a next step without guessing

Offer Turn this into an editable next step for a selected question. Start with plain wording that identifies the question; do not turn an answer into an instruction or infer a medical action. The person edits the words and may add a date/contact. It starts To do and is a separate item. The question and original visit remain unchanged.

## A4 — Review and share by choice

Review what happened and what comes next shows selected questions with their outcomes, deliberately selected personal notes, visible requests and deliberately selected next steps. The title/date are included as appropriate. It excludes private source context and history. Copy, download and print use the exact displayed text and do not save, send, mark contacted or mark done. Clipboard failure offers download.

## A5 — Stop and continue

The ordinary saved visit remains the storage boundary. A new or changed review is temporary until explicit save. Successful saves retain outcomes, notes, next steps and order across reload, full browser restart and offline use after setup. Failed or waiting writes keep the editor and honest status. Existing backup and transfer carry the saved review as normal visit data. Two tabs cannot silently overwrite newer work.

## A6 — Carry unfinished work forward

Still unclear and Not discussed items appear in the existing follow-up and next-appointment choices. Choosing a later visit copies only what is selected, with fresh identities. The original outcome, answer and history remain. Nothing is automatically closed when a new copy is made.

## A7 — Evidence and limits

Deliver plain-language requirements, twelve fictional journeys, a twelve-or-more-section presentation with speaker notes and actual screenshots, updated help, model/browser tests, a requirement map, current-source regression and an extracted verified release. Keep all previous checks. No participant, usefulness, physical-device, native Android/iOS or screen-reader result is claimed.
