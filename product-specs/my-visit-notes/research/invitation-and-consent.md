## Invitation

We are trying a tool called My Visit Notes. It helps people prepare questions and keep them visible during an appointment. We would like to learn whether it helps more than paper or a phone notes app.

We are looking for autistic adults who already prepare appointment notes. You do not need to share a diagnosis record or medical history. We will use made-up appointments. The planned session takes about 30–45 minutes, with breaks or a different format if wanted. This duration is a planning estimate.

You can use your usual communication aids and bring someone you choose. You can skip a question or stop at any time. You do not have to explain why.

## Before deciding

You would try two made-up tasks: one using your usual notes and one using the new app. We would ask what worked, what was hard, and which method you would choose. The app does not provide treatment or medical advice. There may be no direct benefit to you.

We would write down task results and comments using a session code. We would not record audio, video, your screen, or real appointment information. You can decline to share your notes. We will agree what can be included in the report.

## Check understanding and ask permission

Offer this information in a format the person can use. Ask if they want anything explained. A helper may support understanding and communication; do not treat the helper's agreement as the participant's agreement.

Record the person's answer to each item, including “no” or “not yet”:

- I understand the session uses fictional tasks and that I can stop.
- I agree to take part.
- I agree to written notes about task results.
- I agree to my comments being paraphrased without my name in the report.
- Optional: I agree to preparation time being recorded.

If required agreement is absent or unclear, do not start. Review the applicable research requirements with the responsible organiser before recruitment; this draft is not an ethics approval.
