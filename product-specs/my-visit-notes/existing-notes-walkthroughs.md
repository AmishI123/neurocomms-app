# Existing notes — fictional walkthroughs

These examples are invented test material, not participant accounts or medical advice.

## 1. Prepare from scattered notes

Open Bring existing notes. Paste:

> What happens at the next visit?
> Ask the office for the appointment address.
> I want to keep my family situation private.

Select the first line and choose Make this a question. Select the second and choose Make this a next step. Select the third and choose Keep as a private note. The words do not change. You can edit each chosen passage before continuing.

Choose A new temporary visit. Review all three items. Confirm only if replacing the open draft is acceptable. The question starts Open, the next step starts To do, and the private passage goes into private preparation notes. Save on this device if you want to return later.

The source remains in Bring existing notes until you clear it or close the page. Visit backups include the confirmed items, not the unselected source or any unfinished choices.

## 2. Add to an earlier visit without disturbing today's draft

Save an example visit. Start a new draft with a title, but do not save it. Open Bring existing notes, choose a passage, and select the earlier saved visit as the destination. Review and confirm. The earlier record gains the passage. Today's open draft remains unchanged and unsaved.

If another browser tab changes saved data before confirmation, the app stops the write. Your source and choices remain available. Keep a source download and a backup of the open draft before reloading. There is no automatic merging of competing edits.

## 3. A repeated question

Choose wording already present in the destination visit. Review shows Skip because matching text exists. The app ignores whitespace differences for this check but does not infer meaning. Nothing is deleted. If you really need two copies, check Add matching passage anyway and confirm. Otherwise return and choose different material.

## 4. Keep work after saving fails

If additions were made to the open saved visit but automatic saving fails, the updated draft stays open. Download a full backup. Do not repeat the additions to retry saving: the chosen list has already been cleared, and the additions are in the open draft.

If the destination is another saved visit and writing fails, that record stays unchanged. The source and chosen list remain. Resolve the storage problem, return to the working area, review again, and confirm once.

## 5. Share only what you choose

After confirming the example passages, open After the visit. Review the selected summary. The personal next step is not included until its sharing box is checked. Private preparation notes are never included. Preview before downloading, copying or printing. A complete backup is different: it includes private notes and must be kept privately.

## 6. Return offline and recover

Save a visit and wait for the app to say offline files are ready. Reopen the app without a connection and open the saved visit. Existing-notes intake also works offline once its app files have been cached. If browser data was cleared, restore a previously downloaded visit or library backup. Without a backup, the app has no server copy.

Prepare another visit lets you choose questions and personal next steps from a saved visit. It does not carry private preparation notes, answer notes or the source working area into the new visit.
