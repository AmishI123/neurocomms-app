# Open questions

These questions remain after the initial source pass. They are the work that must happen before choosing one product to build.

## Who needs the product?

- Which people want the product themselves, and which people would be expected to use it by someone else?
- What communication methods does each person already use?
- What happens when the person cannot use speech, typing, reading, or a device at that moment?
- Who controls the device, account, vocabulary, data, and settings?

## What is the exact moment of difficulty?

- Does the barrier occur before an interaction, during it, or after it?
- Is the barrier caused by the environment, the information, the device, the other person, the task, or several together?
- What does the person do now to solve it?
- How much time and effort does the proposed product add?

## Does the proposed support preserve control?

- Can the person refuse, change, or remove a suggestion?
- Can the person communicate something unexpected rather than selecting a predefined answer?
- Can the person communicate privately?
- Can the person review what will be shared before it is shared?
- Does the product create a record that a caregiver, school, employer, or provider could use against the person?

## Evidence gaps

- Find more direct adult and self-advocate evidence in Down syndrome.
- Find more AAC-user-led research, including people with different motor, vision, hearing, language, and access needs.
- Find adult workplace and higher-education tasks in dyslexia rather than relying on general reading advice.
- Separate autistic people who use AAC from broader autism communication evidence.
- Gather primary studies on aphasia and keep its acquired condition context separate.
- Compare English-language evidence with other languages and service systems.

## Product questions

- Which product can be tested safely without clinical treatment, child intervention, device prescription, or sensitive health data?
- Which product has a clear success measure such as “the person can express a new message,” “the person can confirm understanding,” or “the person can complete a document task”?
- Which product still works if the internet, battery, caregiver, teacher, or clinician is unavailable?
- Which product has a realistic owner who can keep service, device, or provider information current?
- Which product would people choose to use after the first trial?
