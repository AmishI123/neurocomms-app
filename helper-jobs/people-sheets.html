For the volunteer nearby

You are the adult close by.

A helper is doing a real job with a kid. You stay about an arm’s length away, so the helper is never alone with a kid. You don’t need the tablet.

Do

  • Stay close enough to step in. Keep the kid and the helper in sight.
  • Let the helper do the job, at their pace. Wait before you step in.
  • Check the snack card with them. If it says no snack, no food. If unsure, no snack.
  • You decide at roads and crossings. Say “go” when it is safe.
  • Say what went well. “You checked the card first. Great.”

Don’t

  • Don’t ask a helper to give bathroom or changing help. That is yours.
  • Don’t leave them alone together, even for a minute. Swap with another volunteer first.
  • Don’t talk over the helper or finish their sentences.
  • Don’t treat Stop as a problem. Stopping is always okay.

If the helper presses Help or waves: go to them first. The lead’s phone buzzes too. Then tell the lead what happened, in a few words.

For families

Helper Jobs at Sunday outings

Adults from Pragnya help at the kids’ Sunday outings. Helper Jobs gives each of them a real job (snacks, walking, clean-up, play) with picture steps on a shared tablet, and a volunteer always close by.

Keeping kids safe

  • A helper is never alone with a kid. A named volunteer stays close.
  • Snacks only from your child’s snack card. If the card says no snack, no food.
  • Helpers never give bathroom or changing help.
  • Every kid is counted back at the end, before the outing finishes.

Your child’s details

  • Helpers see a first name, a picture letter and up to three tips you agree to.
  • Allergies and your consent are seen only by the coordinator.
  • No photo of your child without your yes. You can take it back any time.
  • Old records are cleared after the days the coordinator sets.

Questions, or want to change your answers? Talk to the outing coordinator. This is an early test, and we want to hear what you think.
