Helper Jobs · practice Sunday

Try the whole day in 20 minutes

Four adults, one tablet, one phone. Use the made-up people already in the app: no real kids, no real names. Stop the timer whenever something goes wrong and write it down.

Who plays whom

LeadRuns the board on the tablet, keeps the phone in a pocket.
Helper (Asha)Uses the tablet like a helper would. Reads slowly, asks for help once.
Volunteer (Jordan)Stands next to the helper. Plays “the kid” too.
CoordinatorWatches, keeps time, fills in the sheet on page 2.

The script

  1. 0–3 min
    Set up. Coordinator: Start again with the made-up example. Lead: choose a PIN, tick who is here, start the live board. Pair the lead’s phone and send a test buzz.
  2. 3–7 min
    A real job. Lead: match Asha with Snack Helper, Eli, and Jordan nearby. Asha: tap her picture, do the steps, tap Read aloud once, finish and pick a face.
  3. 7–10 min
    Help. Asha starts Clean-Up Crew and taps Help. Lead’s phone is locked in a pocket: does it buzz? Lead taps I’m here on the phone and writes a short note.
  4. 10–13 min
    The day. Lead taps Next part on the day plan. Asha tries a practice run of Play Partner. Asha taps I want to stop, then comes back.
  5. 13–16 min
    The tablet stops. Turn the tablet off for 2 minutes. Lead hands out a paper job card and writes the match on the paper sheet. Turn it back on: is everything still there?
  6. 16–20 min
    Wrap up. Lead: mark every kid back or home with family, then finish. Everyone: two minutes of feedback in the app. Coordinator: open the Sunday report.

Practice Sunday · what we noticed

Ready for the first Sunday?

  • The lead’s locked phone buzzed within a few seconds of Help.
  • Read aloud was loud enough to hear in a noisy room, in every helper’s language.
  • Asha found her picture and her job without anyone pointing.
  • The screen stayed on, and could be read outside.
  • The paper job cards and match sheet were printed and in the bag.
  • After the tablet was turned off and on, nothing was lost.
  • Wrap-up would not finish until every kid was marked.
