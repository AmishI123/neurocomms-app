A checklist for the night before, how to keep helpers inside the app, and a sheet for noticing what happens on the test Sundays.

Helper Jobs · for the volunteer lead and coordinator

Set up the tablet

About fifteen minutes, the day before. Then the tablet is ready, the lead’s phone buzzes for Help, and there is a paper backup.

The night before

  • Charge the tablet and pack the charger and a power bank.
  • Choose the two PINs: one for the lead, a different one for the coordinator.
  • Check the people: every helper and kid has a yes recorded. New kids start as “no snack”.
  • Pair the lead’s phone, turn on “Buzz even when locked”, and send a test buzz.
  • Turn on the laptop backup on the coordinator’s laptop.
  • Print the paper backup: one job card per job, and the match sheet.
  • Once, before the first Sunday: run the 20-minute practice Sunday (Coordinator tools → Paper backup).
  • Turn on helper-only mode (Coordinator tools → This tablet).

iPad: keep helpers in the app

  1. Open Helper Jobs in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access: turn it on and set a passcode only the lead knows.
  3. Open Helper Jobs from the home screen. Press the top (or Home) button three times, then Start.
  4. To leave: press the button three times again and type the passcode.

Android: keep helpers in the app

  1. Install the Helper Jobs app (the APK from the coordinator), or open Helper Jobs in Chrome and tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
  2. Search Settings for App pinning (or Screen pinning), turn it on, and turn on Ask for PIN before unpinning.
  3. In the app: Coordinator tools → This tablet → Pin Helper Jobs to this screen. In Chrome: open the recent apps, tap the Helper Jobs icon, then Pin.
  4. To leave: hold Back and Overview together, then unlock.

On the day

  • Brightness up. Screen lock off, or 30 minutes.
  • Volume up, so read aloud can be heard.
  • Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot, for the lead’s phone and the backup.

If something goes wrong

  • Hand out the paper job cards and use the match sheet.
  • Help is the same: wave or call your volunteer.
  • Restore the laptop’s locked copy on another tablet.

For the three test Sundays

What we notice

Write what happens, not who. No names, no kid details. Five to ten lines is plenty.

Five questions for after the outing

  1. Did each helper have a real job most of the time?
  2. Did anyone need Help? How fast did a person get there?
  3. Was there a moment the tablet got in the way?
  4. Did any snack, road or bathroom moment feel unsafe?
  5. What is one thing to change before next Sunday?

The answers also go into the app: Live board → Volunteer feedback, or at wrap-up. The coordinator can download them, with no names.
