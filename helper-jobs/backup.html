If the tablet breaks

  1. Download a locked copy here.
  2. On another tablet, open Helper Jobs and choose a coordinator PIN.
  3. Coordinator tools → Restore from a copy. Choose the file and type the words.

The copy is locked on the tablet before it is sent. The server keeps only the newest locked copy, for up to 14 days without use. This page keeps nothing on this laptop until you download.
