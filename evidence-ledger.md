## Discovery and exclusions

Initial landscape covered institutes, startups and community priorities. Deeper discovery used bounded 2024–2026 queries for communication/AAC, sensory school environments, ADHD parent-support and organizational tools, dyslexia reading assistance, and biological models. Communication and sensory agents each pursued distinct dependent source reads; the coordinator checked consequential claims, alternative populations, feasibility and rules.

Supplementary leads not used as central report evidence: Jellow 2022 pilot (older Indian AAC novelty counterexample, agent indexed primary access); Alper et al. 2025 child XR ethics interviews (primary abstract verified, full page failed); Kelly et al. 2025 playground co-design (agent indexed primary text, full PDF failed); 2023 central executive training trial (older primary evidence, not needed after the 2025 comparator study); MuseGO and SENSHOME prototypes (agent official descriptions, not needed after the 2026 auditory study).

Organoid biology searches identified a 2025 extracellular-vesicle paper but direct substantive text was not reliably available. GEO GSE271853 appeared in official search results with public status November 12, 2025, but direct access returned CAPTCHA. Neither is used to promise a ready-to-analyze dataset or a wet-lab mechanism. Amar Seva Sangam's EI-app story remained inaccessible after two attempts in the earlier scan and was not retried here. Same-name unrelated ParentCoach projects were excluded.

## Stop decision

Enough primary evidence supports the two proposed directions, existing-product caveats, negative findings and study sketches. Additional broad searches would be redundant for choosing a direction. The remaining gaps depend on the exact PRGN population, available mentorship and chosen comparison. This is not an exhaustive systematic review, not a completed empirical paper, and not proof of untouched global novelty.
