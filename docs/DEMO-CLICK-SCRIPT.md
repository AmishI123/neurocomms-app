# Sunday demo: click-by-click script

About 8 minutes of live demo, told as four moments: **Start, Come back, Say what you need, Find your people**. Fits sections 3–4 of
[`PRESENTATION-RUNBOOK-SUNDAY.md`](PRESENTATION-RUNBOOK-SUNDAY.md).

**Link:** https://mehtama1234.github.io/neuro-divergent/
**Backup:** from `communication-tool/`, run `npm start`, open http://127.0.0.1:8765

## Before you start (the night before and again 15 minutes before)

1. Open the link in **Chrome**, on the laptop you will present from. Reload twice so the newest version loads.
2. Display & privacy: leave text size Normal unless the room is large; then pick Large.
3. Start from a clean slate: Tools → **Erase all local data**, or use a fresh Chrome profile.
4. Keep the backup video open in another tab.

The demo uses the built-in made-up example, so there is nothing to paste.

## The script

### 1. Landing: what this is — 30 seconds
- The landing page opens by itself (or click **What is this?** in the header).
- **Point at:** the four tiles (Start, Come back, Say what you need, Find your people), then "Talk or type, everywhere".
- **Say:** "It helps with four moments where things stall. Nothing leaves this device, and nothing happens unless you choose it."

### 2. Today's energy — 20 seconds
- **Click:** I know how it works — skip this. On Home, under "How much energy do you have today?", **click A little**.
- **Say:** "On a low-energy day it asks for less. Study will show just one step, and stopping early still counts."

### 3. Start: from instructions to checked steps — 90 seconds
- **Click:** Add my instructions, then **Try a made-up example**, then **Find the tasks**.
- **Point at** a card: the "Looks right" or "Check this" stamp, the quoted line, the reason.
  - "Write a short reflection by Friday": the date has no year.
  - "Optional: create one sketch": optional, so you decide. **Untick it.**
  - "If the due date conflicts…": already left out, because it only applies "if".
- **Click:** Use this plan. **Say:** "Nothing became a task until I said so."

### 4. One step, and a way out when stuck — 45 seconds
- **Point at:** "Low-energy day: just this step." The whole list is hidden today.
- **Click:** I'm stuck. **Point at** the four choices: make it smaller, ask a question about it, see how others began, take a break.
- **Say:** "Being stuck is normal, so the app offers a next move instead of a warning."

### 5. Come back — 45 seconds (the key moment)
- **Click:** Take a break. In *Where I paused* type `Picked the tomato bed. Stopped before writing.`
- **Click:** Save & pause. Home shows "Where you paused" with your words.
- **Click:** Return to paused step, then **Mark this step complete**.
- **Say:** "An interruption doesn't erase where you were."

### 6. Say what you need — 60 seconds
- **Click:** Say (bottom bar). In My card, tap the starter **Please write down the steps.** then **Save card**.
- Scroll to **Ask for a change**: pick *A teacher*, type `Ms Rivera`, tap **More time** and **Steps in writing**, tick **Add "What helps me" from my card**.
- **Point at** the message. **Say:** "A polite request in their own name. It does not add any medical label, and it is never sent for them." Click **Copy message**.

### 7. Find your people — 75 seconds
- **Click:** People. Type a made-up name, `Fern`, then **Go to the rooms**.
- **Point at:** "Rooms for what you love": Drawing, Animals, Music, Games, Making, Stories, each with this week's prompt.
- **Click:** Join room on **Drawing**, then **Make something**. Pick the fish stamp, tap two squares, then **Send to the moderator**.
- **Point at:** "Moderator view: practice only". **Say:** "I’m now playing the trained adult who checks every post. In real use that would be a real person, and nobody sees a post until they approve it." **Click:** Approve in room.
- On Kit's picture, **click Love this**. **Say:** "Kind words only. No likes, no counts, no private messages, no photos, no voice recordings."
- *Optional:* open **Music** and press **Play** on a loop (check the sound first).

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| The example does not load | Click Study, then Try a made-up example again. |
| Old data shows on Home | Use a fresh Chrome profile, or erase local data. |
| Venue Wi-Fi drops | Keep going. The app works offline once loaded. Voice input needs internet, so skip it. |
| Sound does not play in Music | Skip it; say "sound only plays when you press Play". |
| Anything else breaks | Play the backup video and narrate over it. |

## Do not say

- Never call it a diagnostic tool, or say it treats anything.
- Never say young people have used it, or that participants were interviewed.
- Never say it is proven to help.

Say instead: "This is a working prototype built on what the research says is hard. Every example is made up. The next step is co-design and a reviewed study with neurodivergent young people."
