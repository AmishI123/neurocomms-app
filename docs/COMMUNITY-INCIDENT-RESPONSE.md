# Community incident response

This runbook is for a trained human safeguarding and operations team. Engineering automation may preserve evidence and restrict access; it must not make emergency judgments or close P0/P1 cases automatically.

## Severity

- **P0:** imminent danger or credible immediate threat. The safeguarding lead owns the case now; use the jurisdiction-approved emergency escalation procedure and preserve evidence.
- **P1:** grooming, sexual exploitation, self-harm concern, or serious coercion. Restrict exposure, assign a safeguarding lead, preserve evidence, and follow the approved reporting/guardian-contact matrix.
- **P2:** privacy exposure, harassment, or repeated harmful behavior. Contain, review, notify according to policy, and track remediation.
- **P3:** routine rule breach or technical abuse. Moderate, rate-limit, document, and monitor recurrence.

## First 15 minutes

1. Acknowledge the case and record the human owner.
2. Do not copy sensitive content into chat, tickets, analytics, or model prompts.
3. Preserve the minimum evidence needed under the approved legal hold.
4. Restrict the affected account/content only as authorized by policy; do not silently delete evidence.
5. Escalate P0/P1 to the safeguarding lead and follow the jurisdiction matrix. The product is not emergency care.
6. Record actions, timestamps, policy version, and next review time in the case system.

## Recovery and closure

The safeguarding lead confirms the action, notification decision, appeal route, and follow-up owner. Closure requires a reason and must respect retention/legal hold. A child-facing explanation must be safe, clear, and non-retaliatory. After the incident, run a blameless review covering detection, access controls, moderation capacity, provider availability, and whether the product boundary should be narrowed.

## Required tabletop scenarios

Before launch, exercise grooming, imminent danger, self-harm language, doxxing/privacy exposure, moderator account compromise, provider outage, false report/appeal, deletion request under legal hold, backup restore, and an immediate stop-new-activity decision.

No runbook substitutes for a named safeguarding owner, trained responders, legal/privacy review, or an approved pilot protocol.
