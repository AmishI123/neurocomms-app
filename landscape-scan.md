# Institutes, companies, and practical research opportunities

7 September 2026. Initial landscape scan for a high-school student exploring projects with PRGN.

## Scope and evidence limits

The user asked to investigate other institutes and startups without waiting for PRGN details. This bounded scan covers Indian service providers and companies, alongside international advocacy and research. It is weighted toward autism and complex communication needs, not representative of all neurodivergence. Organization pages establish what providers offer or say they address; they do not independently establish effectiveness or how widespread a problem is. This is not a completed deep literature review.

The plan was to discover relevant organizations, check their primary pages, compare overlapping needs and existing solutions, and identify candidate research questions. An update_plan tool was not available. No need to contact organizations or collect child data for this scan.

## Research connection

Choi and colleagues' AACessTalk paper, accepted at CHI 2025, describes a two-week deployment with 11 minimally verbal autistic child-parent pairs. The abstract reports increased conversation and turn-taking using parent guidance and contextual vocabulary recommendations. This supports investigating communication as an interaction involving both partners. It does not establish long-term effectiveness or transfer to Indian multilingual settings. Only the abstract and publication metadata were inspected.

## Candidate projects

1. Communication-partner support: investigate whether brief guidance helps adults leave room for child-initiated expression with an existing AAC system. Outcomes could include child initiation, successful repair of misunderstandings, and user preference. Novelty and an appropriate study design remain to be established.
2. Home–institute handoff: prototype a brief shared update about preferences, communication, and supports; first evaluate adult usability using fictional scenarios. Compare time required and information missed.
3. Sensory environment: prototype room-level noise/light mapping alongside voluntary comfort feedback. Test sensor accuracy and the usability of a room audit before any participant study. Environmental measurements alone cannot determine a child's internal state.

Communication-partner support and sensory environments are promising directions to explore further, based on fit with the user's intended paper/prototype. They are not demonstrated novel gaps.
