# Leave the appointment with a clear record of what happened

Status: delivered. This goal builds on the approved next-appointment release.

## Outcome

After a visit, someone can review each question, choose a plain outcome, write a private answer in their own words, turn a question into an editable next step, and create a short private review. Nothing is marked done or shared automatically. The review can be saved, resumed offline, transferred and used to prepare the next visit.

## Full delivery

1. Plain outcomes: Answered, Partly answered, Still unclear and Not discussed.
2. Personal notes are clearly labelled as personal and not confirmed instructions.
3. A chosen question can become an editable next step with optional date and contact; the app does not interpret the answer.
4. A review shows only deliberately selected questions, notes, requests and next steps. Download/copy does not send, share, save or mark work done.
5. Save halfway through, return offline, transfer devices and continue without losing words or duplicating entries.
6. Unclear items can be carried to the next appointment; earlier records remain unchanged.
7. Deliver requirements, twelve fictional journeys, executive/conference HTML, help, tests, an acceptance audit and an extracted verified release.

## Finish line
