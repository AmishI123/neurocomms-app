# Know what still needs doing after the visit

Status: completed as an engineering release. All 162 current-source checks passed. The 137-file archive is verified after extraction, including the full follow-up journey and nine HTML documents. See product-specs/my-visit-notes/followup-acceptance-audit.md and the matching release manifest/verification for evidence. User benefit remains unverified.

## Outcome

Someone can reopen the app after several appointments, find unfinished questions and next steps, choose one to focus on, prepare a message or call note, record what happened and carry unresolved work into the next visit without losing its source or exposing private notes.

## Required delivery

1. One read-only overview of unfinished items across saved visits, with links to their original notes.
2. Explicit, reversible follow-up progress: contacted, waiting for a reply, done or no longer needed. Optional dates and private progress notes, retained with the item.
3. One deliberately chosen focus item. No automatic urgency, overdue warnings or medical importance ranking.
4. An editable message/call-note draft assembled only from explicitly selected items and context. Exact preview before copy/download; no automatic sending or automatic status changes.
5. Selected carry-forward from one or more visits, with fresh item identities, visible source information and unchanged originals. Copied items are independent, not silently synchronised.
6. Working offline software, migration and backup support, detailed requirements, fictional walkthroughs, executive/conference HTML with speaker notes, acceptance evidence and a verified release.

## Completion condition

Exercise the complete two-appointment journey through waiting, reopening and closing or carrying forward an item. Check failure, cancellation, concurrent tabs, private-output boundaries, original/copy identity and recovery. Run all existing regression gates plus new model/browser checks; verify the extracted archive and rendered presentation. No participants are required. User benefit remains unverified.

## Boundaries

No accounts, cloud storage, automatic messages, notifications, medical advice or automatic document interpretation. A sent/copied message is not proof of contact. Existing saved visits, preparations, recoverable items and private backups must remain supported.
